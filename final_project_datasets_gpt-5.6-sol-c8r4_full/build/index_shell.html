About missing data. Nothing here has been dropped for being incomplete. Instead, any variable that is more than 10% missing is marked (x% missing) next to its name, and every variable's exact completeness is in the codebook on its dataset page.

Missing values matter because lm() and glm() silently drop any row missing any variable in the model. Add one 40%-missing predictor to a model and you can lose 40% of your sample without being told. So: prefer the variables with no missingness marker; if you do want a marked variable, check what your model's complete-case N actually is with nrow(model.frame(your_model)), report the number of rows you lost, and flag it as a limitation.

A note on derived outcomes. Several datasets ship both a score and its yes/no version of the same thing (for example systolic_bp and hypertension, or height_for_age_z and stunted). Pick one as your outcome. Never put the other one in as a predictor: it is the same measurement, and your model will fit perfectly and mean nothing.
