ANOVA hypotheses
One-way ANOVA (analysis of variance) tests whether k group means are all equal, with one F test instead of many pairwise t-tests.
Null hypothesis

$H_0:\ \mu_\text{prim} = \mu_\text{lsec} = \mu_\text{hsec} = \mu_\text{univ}$

Alternative hypothesis

$H_1:$ at least one $\mu$ differs

The F statistic | A Large F is evidence of true difference
F ratio

$F = \dfrac{\color{#1f7a4d}{MS_\text{between}}}{\color{#8c7ab8}{MS_\text{within}}}$

Mean-square formulas

$\color{#1f7a4d}{MS_\text{between}} = \dfrac{\sum n_j(\bar y_j-\bar y)^2}{k-1}$
$\color{#8c7ab8}{MS_\text{within}} = \dfrac{\sum\sum(y_{ij}-\bar y_j)^2}{N-k}$

In plain language

$\color{#1f7a4d}{MS_\text{between}}=\dfrac{\text{group means from grand mean}}{\text{group df}}$
$\color{#8c7ab8}{MS_\text{within}}=\dfrac{\text{people from own group mean}}{\text{within df}}$

PREVEND calculation

$\color{#1f7a4d}{MS_B} = 115{,}041 / 3 = 38{,}347$
$\color{#8c7ab8}{MS_W} = 259{,}469 / 496 = 523$
$F = 38{,}347 / 523 = \color{#b5451f}{73.3}$

How to do it in R
R
fit <- aov(rfft ~ education, data = rsr::prevend)
summary(fit)
#>              Df Sum Sq Mean Sq F value Pr(>F)
#> education     3 115041   38347    73.3 <2e-16 ***
#> Residuals   496 259469     523
Mean RFFT scores differed significantly across education groups, F(3, 496) = 73.3, p < .001. The test tells us at least one true mean differs.

  1. How many groups are being compared?
  2. What are the between and within df?
  3. Is the omnibus test significant?
  4. Write one sentence reporting the result.
Solutions: 3 groups; df = 2 and 27; yes, p = .0159; plant weights differed by treatment group, F(2, 27) = 4.85, p = .016.
Tukey follow-up
ANOVA tells us that at least one education group differs. Tukey’s HSD then checks which pairs differ, while adjusting for the fact that we are making several comparisons. Here, all six pairs differ.
