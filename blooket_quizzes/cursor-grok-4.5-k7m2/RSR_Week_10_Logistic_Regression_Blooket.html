Answer Key · Week 10

Blooket / classroom key · cursor-grok-4.5-k7m2
Correct option is marked first. Keep this page out of the student Blooket set.
1B Binary yes/no outcome (cured or not).
Continuous and count outcomes need other models.
2B Logistic regression predicts the probability of the outcome.
3B glm(..., family = binomial)
lm / gaussian is for continuous outcomes.
4B 0.117 is the change in log odds per extra year of age.
ln(p/(1−p)) = −8.82 + 0.117 × age
5C Odds = 0.80 / 0.20 = 4.0
Odds = p / (1 − p)
6B OR = e0.1168 ≈ 1.12
Positive age coefficient → OR > 1
7B OR 0.75 → 25% lower odds among the exposed.
1 − 0.75 = 0.25
8B Misconception: OR 1.12 ≠ +12 percentage points in probability.
It is a 12% change in odds, not probability.
