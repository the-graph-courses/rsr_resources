What does a z-score tell you?

A z-score expresses distance from the mean in units of standard deviation. For example, z = 2 means the value is two standard deviations above the mean.

In the z-score formula below:What does dividing by the standard deviation do?

The numerator gives the raw distance from the mean. Dividing by the standard deviation turns that raw distance into “how many standard deviations away.”

A patient has a blood pressure z-score of +2. What does this mean?

z = +2 means the value is two standard deviations above the mean. It does not mean “2 raw units,” and it does not mean “twice the mean.”

A patient has blood pressure 150. The sample mean is 130, and the standard deviation is 20. What is the z-score?

x = 150  ·  mean = 130  ·  standard deviation = 20
Use z = (x - mean) / standard deviation. Here, (150 - 130) / 20 = 20 / 20 = 1.

Two students take different coding quizzes. Who performed better relative to their own quiz group?

Ravi's z-score is (72 - 60) / 6 = 2. Maya's z-score is (86 - 80) / 12 = 0.5. Even though Maya's raw score is higher, Ravi performed better relative to his own group.

After z-standardizing a normally distributed variable, what is the mean of the standardized values?

After z-standardization, the mean becomes 0. The original mean is the center, and the center has z-score 0.

After z-standardizing a normally distributed variable, what is the standard deviation of the standardized values?

After z-standardization, the standard deviation becomes 1. This is why z-scores are measured in standard-deviation units.

What percentage of values are below z = 0 in a standard normal distribution?

z = 0 is the center of the standard normal distribution. Because the distribution is symmetric, 50% of values are below it and 50% are above it.

A z-table tells us that 15.87% of values are below z = -1. Using symmetry, what percentage of values are above z = +1?

The standard normal distribution is symmetric. The area below z = -1 is the same as the area above z = +1, so the answer is 15.87%.
