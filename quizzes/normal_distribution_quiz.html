Two machines fill medicine bottles. Both average 100 mL, but Machine B is less consistent (it varies more from bottle to bottle). Which statement describes Machine B's distribution?

Both machines share the same average (mean). Machine B varies more, so its standard deviation is larger. That spreads the density curve out horizontally and lowers its peak so the total area stays at 100%.

The shaded region below shows values falling inside a specific interval on a density curve. What does the shaded area represent?

In a density curve, area equals probability. If the shaded area is 0.30, you expect 30% of observations to fall in that interval.

Adult resting heart rate is approximately normal with μ = 70 bpm and σ = 10 bpm. Using the 68-95-99.7 rule, what percentage of adults fall between 60 and 80 bpm?

The interval from 60 to 80 is exactly one standard deviation below to one standard deviation above the mean (70 ± 10). By the rule, this middle region holds roughly 68%.

Exam scores are approximately normal with μ = 70 and σ = 10. What percentage of students scored between 80 and 90?

The gap from 70 to 90 holds 47.5% of scores, and the gap from 70 to 80 holds 34%. Subtracting gives the slice between 80 and 90: 47.5% − 34% = 13.5%.

About 95% of values in a normal distribution fall within 2 standard deviations of the mean. What percentage of values fall outside this interval in total?

If 95% sits inside the interval, the remaining area outside is 100% − 95% = 5%. That 5% splits evenly into 2.5% for each tail.

In a standard normal distribution (μ = 0 and σ = 1), what area is contained between −2 and +1?

From −2 to 0 is half of the 95% interval (47.5%). From 0 to +1 is half of the 68% interval (34%). Adding them gives 47.5% + 34% = 81.5%.
