# Feasibility report: the later datasets

Worked analyses for every outcome offered by datasets 9 to 14 of the catalogue — the
Japanese health-check cohort, rheumatoid arthritis and cIMT, HIV and exercise capacity,
tibial bone strength, Abuja household malaria, and COURAGE ageing and quality of life.
Sixteen documents in all.

Each lives at `analyses/<slug>/<outcome_var>.Rmd` with a knitted `.html` beside it, and
follows `_TEMPLATE.Rmd`: introduction, methods, Table 1, exploratory figure, univariable
models, a paragraph on why adjustment is needed, the multivariable model, an
unadjusted-versus-adjusted merge, diagnostics, discussion with limitations, and a private
feasibility appendix.

*NOT part of a student report.* This file is for the teaching team.

Dataset numbers here follow the catalogue as generated by `build/build_index.py`. They
shift whenever `ORDER` changes; the subtitles inside each `.Rmd` are kept in step.

## Withdrawn after these analyses were fitted

Recorded so the decisions stay reviewable rather than becoming folklore.

- **NHANES adults, US births 2014 and FAMuSS** were removed from the catalogue
  entirely, along with their nine worked analyses. FAMuSS was the weakest: its 595
  shipped rows are an upstream `na.omit()` of 1,397, so every column reads as 0% missing
  while 57% of the study has already been deleted, and with no control group — everybody
  trained — nothing in the file estimates the effect of training. NHANES and US births
  were also the catalogue's only two datasets without a linked source paper.
- **`abi_max`** was withdrawn as an offered outcome for dataset 9, though the column still
  ships. It has no clinical variance: 0 of 876 participants fall below the 0.90 PAD
  threshold, 94.7% sit in the normal 1.00–1.30 band, and there are 41 distinct values on a
  0.01 recording grid. It is uncorrelated with `pwv_max` (r = 0.01) and every association
  runs in the clinically wrong direction — higher BMI, alcohol and exercise all predict
  higher ABI, most plausibly a cuff artefact in thicker limbs. Its diagnostics were the
  cleanest of that dataset's four outcomes, which is exactly what made it misleading. The
  reason is recorded in `EXCLUDED_OUTCOMES` in `build/build_index.py`.

## Errors still present in the shipped catalogue

Factual defects, each verified against the CSV. **Not yet fixed.**

1. **`depression_diagnosis` is listed as both an outcome and a suggested predictor** for
   `ageing_qol_europe`. A student copying the predictor list regresses it on itself.
2. **`data/cimt_rheumatoid.csv` contains one entirely blank record** (`id =
   "control_row1"`, populated only for `id`, `has_ra` and `hypertension_or_hyperchol`). The
   advertised 470 rows are 469 participants plus an empty row, so every completeness
   percentage in that codebook is one row pessimistic.
3. **The `abi_max` caution names the wrong rows.** It says "36 rows (ids 491-526)". The
   affected ids are 491–513 plus 617, 654, 674, 679, 720, 804, 828, 829, 855, 877, 884,
   901 and 903. The missingness itself is uninformative (p = 0.61).
4. **No household identifier ships with `nigeria_malaria_households`,** though the source
   paper's analysis used GEE keyed on it. The published result cannot be reproduced from
   this extract.
5. **`log2_ggt` is listed as a suggested predictor of itself** for dataset 9.

## Fatal traps: variables that are secretly the same variable

The recurring failure across these datasets is a suggested predictor that is an exact or
near-exact function of another suggested variable. In most cases R issues no warning and
the VIF stays below any conventional threshold, so a student following the catalogue's own
predictor list builds a broken model and sees nothing wrong.

- **`tibial_bone_strength`** — `relative_peak_power` is exactly `peak_power_w /
  body_mass_kg`. Both are suggested. Together with body mass the VIFs are 89, 36 and 31.
  Worse, `peak_power_w` is itself the Sayers equation `51.9 × jump_cm + 48.9 × body_mass −
  2007`, recovered by inversion to within 0.005 W, so body mass sits *inside* peak power
  with a fixed coefficient. That is why the adjusted body-mass coefficient comes out
  negative — an artefact the source paper reproduces without explaining.
- **`hiv_lung_6mwt`** — `sgrq_total` is a fixed weighted sum of `sgrq_symptoms`,
  `sgrq_activity` and `sgrq_impacts` (R² = 0.9996, recovering the published 0.16/0.32/0.51
  weights). All four columns are listed adjacently with no warning.
- **`cimt_rheumatoid`** — `ldl_cholesterol` is the Friedewald calculation of the other three
  lipids (r = 0.9987). All four are suggested; together the VIFs reach ~500.
- **`ggt_arterial_stiffness`** — `log2_ggt` is exactly `log2(ggt)` and is suggested as a
  predictor of itself. The four lipids are Friedewald-redundant (VIF up to 24), and
  `systolic_bp`/`diastolic_bp` correlate at 0.90.
- **`nigeria_malaria_households`** — `owns_llin` and `used_llin_previous_night` are exactly
  nested (all 107 users own; all 454 non-owners coded 0). The five `house_*` variables are
  five proxies for one construct with structural zeros.

## Fatal traps: missingness that deletes the comparison

- **`hiv_lung_6mwt` is the worst case in the catalogue.** `cd4_count`,
  `on_antiretroviral` and the viral-load columns exist only for the 297 HIV-positive
  participants. Adding `on_antiretroviral` leaves **zero** HIV-negative participants and
  `lm()` errors; adding `cd4_count` silently cuts the HIV-negative group from 126 to 20,
  more than doubles the HIV standard error and moves p from 0.007 to 0.291. A student would
  conclude HIV is unassociated with walk distance. `mmrc_dyspnoea` is missing for 37% of
  HIV-positive participants and 0% of HIV-negatives.
- **`cimt_rheumatoid`** — `carotid_plaque` is missing for 23.9% of controls versus 1.0% of
  RA cases. The catalogue's flat "8.9% missing" hides that a quarter of the comparison
  group is gone, non-randomly, exactly where `has_ra` is the exposure.

## Fatal traps: separation that does not announce itself

- **`nigeria_malaria_households`** — `house_window × house_wall` produces β = 14.7 with
  SE = 441, driven by 4 participants all positive, and **`glm()` issues no warning**: the
  maximum fitted probability is 0.9999998, just under the `1 - 1e-8` trigger. Standard
  errors and aliased `NA` terms are the reliable detectors here, not the warning.
- **`ageing_qol_europe`** — `country × alcohol_use` gives β = −12.6, SE = 266 (Poland ×
  `frequent_heavy` has 11 people and 0 events); `education == "none_or_informal"` is
  effectively Spain-only (124 of 125).
- **`cimt_rheumatoid`** — banding `age_yrs` causes complete separation: zero plaque events
  among the 50 participants under 40. Age must stay continuous.

## Interpretive traps worth naming in the brief

- **Mediators that improve R² and are therefore irresistible.** `systolic_bp` is the
  difference between "GGT is associated with arterial stiffness" (p = 0.005) and "it is
  not" (p = 0.101) — and baPWV is derived from a cuff pressure waveform, so blood pressure
  is partly the outcome.
- **Symptoms masquerading as causes.** `sadness` and `sleep_difficulty` are diagnostic
  criteria for depression; adding `sadness` lifts Tjur's R² from 0.121 to 0.164 and yields
  OR ≈ 7.1, so it looks like the best finding in the report. For `quality_of_life_score`,
  `body_pain`, `sleep_difficulty` and `sadness` are components of the WHOQOL-AGE construct
  rather than causes of it.
- **Confounding by indication.** `statins` and `antihypertensives` in `cimt_rheumatoid`;
  statin use is concentrated in controls (13.9% versus 4.4%), so `statinsYes` crosses into
  significance on adjustment and reads as a clean anti-inflammatory effect.
- **Majority-class outcome.** `malaria_parasitaemia` is 421 positive versus 181 negative —
  the only such outcome in the catalogue. The events-per-parameter budget is set by the 181
  negatives, not the 421 positives.
- **Power without practical significance.** At n = 5,341 the COURAGE models make trivial
  associations significant; the documents make the point explicitly.

## Where the parameter-budget rule fails

The ten-events-per-parameter heuristic did not protect three of these datasets, and the
documents had to argue their predictor cuts on other grounds:

- **`ageing_qol_europe`** — the 21 suggested predictors cost **39** parameters, not 21.
  Both logistic outcomes still pass the rule (49.4 and 20.1 EPP), so the full model fits
  silently. The cut rests on interpretability, multiplicity (≈2 expected false positives),
  mediation and cell-level thinness.
- **`ggt_arterial_stiffness`** — at N ≈ 883 and 253 events, all 18 predictors pass. The cut
  rests on redundancy alone.
- **`nigeria_malaria_households`** — 14 predictors cost 27 parameters; the binding
  constraint is the 181 negatives, and the design effect of ~2.3 from household clustering
  cuts the effective N to ~265, turning 11.3 negatives per parameter into about 5.

## Clustering and design features ignored by these models

`nigeria_malaria_households` (ICC ≈ 0.28, design effect ≈ 2.3, cluster-robust SEs inflate
1.24× for household-level terms and three coefficients cross 0.05); `ageing_qol_europe`
(sampling weights, strata and clusters, none applied); `hiv_lung_6mwt` (two recruitment
sites, site variable not shipped); `ggt_arterial_stiffness` (health-check attendees are
healthier than the general population, and no medication data ship at all).

## Diagnostics: what actually fails

Failed assumption checks are the norm rather than the exception, which is good teaching
material but should be anticipated in the brief.

- **Transformation helps:** `polar_strength_strain_index` (log fixes both checks and raises
  R² to 0.617); `crp` (log takes residual skew 5.01 → 0.77 and R² 0.098 → 0.192).
- **Transformation actively hurts:** `cimt_total` (log takes skew 0.39 → −1.48); `pwv_max`
  and `log2_ggt` (log fixes neither check).
- **Nothing helps** — point masses no monotone transformation can repair: `crp` is
  left-censored with 42% at the reporting floor of 1; `quality_of_life_score` has 355
  participants on the single value 75.
- **Robust standard errors change a conclusion** in `log2_ggt` (AST p = 0.044 → 0.178).
  Elsewhere HC3 changes nothing.
- **Binned residuals fail in every logistic model here** — 52% inside bounds for
  depression, 70% for hypertension and fatty liver, 84% for malaria, against a 95% target.
  At n = 5,341 this is not a small-bin artefact.
- **Influence that Cook's distance misses:** for `polar_strength_strain_index` the maximum
  Cook's D is only 0.141, but dropping that one participant moves the peak-power
  coefficient 22% and p from 0.010 to 0.029. The leave-one-out refit, not the Cook's panel,
  is what exposes it at n = 142.
