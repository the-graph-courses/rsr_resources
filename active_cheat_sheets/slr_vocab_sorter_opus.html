The six concepts
Predictor x
The variable on the right of the ~. What we use to predict or explain.
Outcome y
The variable on the left. What we are modelling.
Slope b1
The number multiplied by x. How much y changes per +1 in x.
Intercept b0
Predicted y when x = 0. Where the line meets the y-axis.
Residual y − ŷ
Vertical gap from a point to the line. The miss for one observation.
SE of slope SE(b1)
Slope uncertainty: how much b1 would wobble across samples.
